# PCAO-F Seat Opening Campaign Plan

**Book:** *The AI Agent Factory* (https://agentfactory.panaversity.org/)
**Goal:** Get Panaversity students and external candidates to sit PCAO-F (Panaversity Certified Associate: Foundations) when the new proctored seats open.
**Channels:** YouTube, LinkedIn, Instagram
**Team:** 1 video editor, 2 writers (Writer A: English; Writer B: Urdu / Roman Urdu, plus English)
**Plan date:** 28 October 2026. **Seat opening (assumed):** Wednesday 9 December 2026, about 6 weeks out.

---

## 1. Assumptions (check these before Week 1)

1. **Seat date.** The live PCAO-F page says "Proctored PCAO-F sittings begin on September 18." I read the brief's "opening seats in about 6 weeks" as a **new batch of seats** (more capacity, or a new booking window) opening around **9 December 2026**. If the date is different, move the whole calendar by the same amount. Before launch, update the "Schedule Your Exam" line on the PCAO-F page so it matches the campaign. Otherwise external candidates will see two different dates.
2. **Fees are not announced.** The book says the external proctoring fee and the fee for a third or later attempt are "not announced yet." Content must not give a price until Panaversity publishes one. Until then, use "two free attempts if you are a Panaversity student" and "external candidates pay only the proctoring fee."
3. **On-camera talent.** Zia Khan or another Panaversity faculty member can record 2 to 3 hours of raw footage a week. The editor edits and does not present. If nobody can present, use the fallback in Section 8.
4. **Design.** Nobody on the team is a designer. LinkedIn carousels and Instagram carousels are made from 3 to 4 Canva/Figma templates the editor builds in Week 0. Writers then fill them in.
5. **Tracking.** Every link goes to the PCAO-F page with UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium=social`, `utm_campaign=pcaof_dec26`, `utm_content=<asset-id>`). Seat requests and sample-exam starts can be counted from Panaversity accounts.
6. **Language split.** Urdu/Roman Urdu content mostly reaches Panaversity/PIAIC students in Pakistan and the diaspora. English content does more of the work with external candidates, such as global professionals, consultants and ops/marketing people.

## 2. Facts the campaign rests on (from the book)

Every claim in the content has to trace back to one of these. Sources: `certifications-pcao-f` (https://agentfactory.panaversity.org/docs/certifications/pcao-f) and `certifications-overview` (https://agentfactory.panaversity.org/docs/certifications).

| Fact | Message use |
| --- | --- |
| PCAO-F tests **judgment**: using AI, evaluating its output, governing where it operates. It does not test building. Written for people who use AI daily without writing integration code (ops, marketing, PM, comms, teaching, consulting, analysis). | Opens the door to non-coders and external professionals |
| Covers **every CCAO-F objective at the published weights**, then goes "one level up": vendor-neutral, with Claude and ChatGPT side by side | "Same blueprint, one level up" |
| Cut score **720 / 1,000**, same as CCAO-F | Credibility |
| 7 domains. **Output Evaluation & Validation is the largest (21%)**. Prompting is only 14%. Evaluation, Workflow (16%) and Governance (15%) together = 52% | Hook: "The biggest part of this AI exam isn't prompting" |
| **Panaversity students: two free attempts.** External candidates pay only a proctoring fee (not announced). No enrolment needed to sit it | Main CTA for each audience |
| **Free sample exam**: same number of questions and time limit, does not use an attempt, score reports saved to your account | Low-friction first step |
| Study guide = free crash courses (Just Delegate It, AI Fluency, AI Prompting in 2026, Workflow Design & Diagnosis, Governance Risk & Responsible Use, etc.) with flashcards and MCQs | Prep content and "study with us" series |
| Path: **PCAO-F → PCAR-F → FDE Internship Program**. Students and external candidates are held to the same standard, and external candidates qualify too | Bigger reason to start |
| **FTE Startup Founders Program project work starts the day you pass PCAO-F** | Immediate payoff for passing |
| An Anthropic CCAO-F attempt costs $99, and failures bring waits of 14/30/90 days. PCAO-F is the cheaper readiness check | Rational reason to sit PCAO-F first |
| The Panaversity route helps with Anthropic registration (organisational account required, no individual sign-up) for internship participants | Secondary, for people already eyeing CCAO-F |

### Guardrails (claims the content must never make)

- Do not call PCAO-F an Anthropic credential or "Claude Certified." The book says plainly that PCAO-F is Panaversity's readiness gate and that **CCAO-F is the credential employers verify with Anthropic.**
- Do not say PCAO-F alone qualifies someone for the internship. **Both PCAO-F and PCAR-F** are needed.
- Do not quote fees for externals or for a third attempt.
- Do not imply Anthropic requires or endorses PCAO-F.
- Do not promise jobs or salaries. The book itself says the credentials are new and have "no mature salary data."

## 7. Team workload (who does what each week)

Rough sustainable load. Week 6 is the peak, so Week 5's assets should be finished by the end of Week 4.

| Role | Weekly output | Hours (approx.) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Video editor** | 1 long video (EN or UR main) + 1 secondary-language version/cut, 3 Shorts reused as Reels and LinkedIn clips, thumbnails, carousel layouts from templates | ~30–35 h. Week 6 live adds ~6 h (setup, clips) |
| **Writer A (EN)** | 1 YouTube script (EN), 3 LinkedIn text posts, LinkedIn carousel copy, YouTube descriptions/titles (EN), Shorts captions (EN), FAQ answers | ~20–25 h |
| **Writer B (Urdu + EN)** | Urdu/Roman Urdu script or adaptation (main in Weeks 3 and 5), Instagram carousel copy and captions, Roman Urdu LinkedIn posts, Urdu Shorts captions/subtitles, glossary upkeep, comment replies in Urdu | ~20–25 h |
| **Presenter (Zia / faculty)** | 2–3 h recording, plus posting from a personal LinkedIn profile | 3–4 h |

**Workflow per long video:** Writer drafts the script (Mon) → fact check against the PCAO-F page and the guardrails (Tue) → record (Wed) → editor cuts long + Shorts (Thu–Fri) → writers write captions/descriptions (Fri) → publish the following week. Working one week ahead from Week 1 means a missed day doesn't push the launch.

**Translation, not transcreation, is the trap.** Writer B adapts examples to local context (a Pakistani SME, a freelancing client, a school) instead of translating the English script line by line.

## 8. Risks and fallbacks

| Risk | Fallback |
| --- | --- |
| No presenter available | Screen recordings of the study guide pages, mock-sample walkthroughs and slide videos with voice-over by Writer B (Urdu) or Writer A (EN). Faceless formats work for "study session" content |
| Editor overloaded | Drop the secondary-language long video in Weeks 2 and 4. Keep Shorts in both languages. Shorts move more people than a second long video |
| Seat date slips | Shift Weeks 5–7 only. Weeks 1–4 (awareness and prep) still build sample-exam usage, which is the leading indicator anyway |
| External fee announced mid-campaign | Add it to the W5 booking video and carousel. Re-check every asset that says "not announced" |
| Content implies PCAO-F = Anthropic certification | Guardrail checklist (Section 2) is part of the fact-check step for every asset |
| Practice questions look like leaked items | Writers write original judgment scenarios. Never claim they come from the real bank |

## 9. Measurement

**North-star:** PCAO-F seats booked (students via teachers + externals via paid one-on-one).

**Leading indicator:** free sample-exam starts and completions. These are the clearest sign that someone intends to sit.

| Metric | Source | Target (directional; set a baseline in Week 1) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sample-exam starts from campaign UTMs | Panaversity accounts + UTM | Week-over-week growth from Week 2 |
| Seat requests / bookings | Exam scheduler | Split by student vs external, and by language of the referring asset |
| YouTube watch time and CTR to the PCAO-F page | YouTube Studio | Compare EN vs Urdu per week |
| LinkedIn link clicks and follower growth (S2) | LinkedIn analytics | Best indicator for external reach |
| IG saves and Story link taps | IG Insights | Saves show study intent |

**Weekly 30-minute review (every Monday):** look at the top 3 assets by sample-exam starts, make more of that format the following week, and cut the weakest format. In Week 4, decide whether the Week 6 live leads in English or Urdu based on which language drove more sample starts.

The targets are left as "set a baseline in Week 1" on purpose. No historical numbers were available for this plan, and inventing them would mislead.
