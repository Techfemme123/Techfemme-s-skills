## 1. Goal, audience and language mix

**Goal: certifications.** Get candidates to sit the **free PCAO-F sample**, then **book a proctored seat** in the new window. Each piece has one primary CTA. Early pieces point to the sample. Booking CTAs start in Week 4.

**Two audiences, two different offers:**

| Audience | What's true for them (from `/docs/certifications`) | Main fear / question | Language |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Panaversity students** (PIAIC community, enrolled learners) | Two free attempts per exam; arrange the seat with their teacher | "Am I ready? What if I fail?" | Roman Urdu + Urdu script, with English for anything technical |
| **B. External candidates** (working professionals in ops, marketing, PM, consulting, teaching, analysis, plus career-changers in Pakistan and abroad) | "You do not have to study with Panaversity to sit the exams." They pay a proctoring fee (TBA) and get one-on-one proctoring | "What is this, why would I sit it instead of the Anthropic exam, and is it worth my time?" | English (LinkedIn, YouTube global); Roman Urdu for Pakistani professionals on Instagram |

The PCAO-F page names its target candidate: someone "who already works with AI every day without writing the integration code. Operations, marketing, project management, communications, teaching, consulting, analysis." That gives the external campaign its audience. It isn't aimed at developers.

**Language mix (rough):** about 50% English, 35% Roman Urdu, 15% Urdu script. All exam announcements exist in **English and in Urdu script as faithful translations**, because the facts must not drift. Educational pieces are **adaptations**, not translations.

**Assumptions:**
- Seats open around 9 Dec 2026, and the campaign runs Weeks 1–6 (2 Nov – 13 Dec). There's a short Week 7 tail after opening.
- The video editor can deliver **one long YouTube video per week**, or one long video plus a lighter talking-head video in some weeks, plus **3–4 vertical cuts** from it.
- Each writer can deliver about 5–6 short pieces or 1 long piece plus 3 short pieces a week. Writer B writes all Urdu and Roman Urdu and reviews any Urdu script before it's published.
- On camera: a Panaversity teacher (ideally Zia Khan or a course instructor) for 30–60 minutes of recording a week. **[verify availability]**
- No paid ads budget is assumed.

## 2. Pillars in play

Three of the five book pillars carry this campaign. Pillar 1 (The Line) is used only as a hook.

### P5. Getting Paid: the FDE career (primary)
- **Hub:** Certifications: Proof You Can Carry In — https://agentfactory.panaversity.org/docs/certifications, plus PCAO-F — https://agentfactory.panaversity.org/docs/certifications/pcao-f
- **Why:** This is the decision-stage content. It explains the path, the gate, what it costs, and why PCAO-F comes before CCAO-F.
- **Clusters:** the path (PCAO-F → PCAR-F → FDE Internship → optional CCAO-F/CCAR-F); why the gate exists; PCAO-F vs CCAO-F; the six mistakes; booking how-to for students vs external candidates; "an honest limit".

### P2. AI Fluency for Everyone (the study content)
- **Hub:** Foundations — https://agentfactory.panaversity.org/docs/foundations. The PCAO-F study guide is built from it.
- **Why:** PCAO-F tests judgment, and the courses that teach it are the Foundations crash courses. This pillar is searchable and works well for beginners and in Urdu.
- **Clusters, mapped to the exam's domain weights (CCAO-F guide v1.0, as quoted on the PCAO-F page):**
  - Output Evaluation & Validation, 21%: AI Fluency, Just Delegate It, Governance
  - Workflow Integration & Solution Design, 16%: Workflow Design & Diagnosis
  - Governance, Risk & Responsible Use, 15%: Governance crash course
  - Prompting & Task Execution, 14%: AI Prompting in 2026
  - Product & Model Selection, 12%, and Configuration & Knowledge, 12%: ChatGPT & Claude Quick Reference, What AI Actually Is, Skills & Connectors
  - Troubleshooting & Optimization, 10%: Workflow Design & Diagnosis, General Agents on the Web

### Zia Tutor as the study companion (cross-pillar)
- **Hub:** https://agentfactory.panaversity.org/docs/ecosystem/zia-tutor-ai and the "Layer 4: Zia Tutor AI" section of https://agentfactory.panaversity.org/docs/how-to-learn-from-this-book
- **Why:** It's free, it runs inside the free tier of the AI app the learner already uses, and it remembers progress. It answers "I don't have a teacher" for external candidates. Zia Tutor is in **Beta 1** (per `/docs/ecosystem/ecosystem-concept`), so say so.

### P1. The Line (hook only)
- **Hub:** https://agentfactory.panaversity.org/docs/what-you-carry-in. The certifications page opens from it: "what you carry between companies matters more than what you leave behind."
- **Used for:** 1–2 LinkedIn openers for external professionals.
