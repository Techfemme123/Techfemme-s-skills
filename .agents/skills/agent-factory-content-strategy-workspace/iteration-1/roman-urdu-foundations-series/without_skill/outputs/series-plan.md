# "AI Seekho, Asaan Zubaan Mein": Foundations Shorts Series Plan

**Kitab:** The AI Agent Factory (Panaversity), https://agentfactory.panaversity.org/
**Source track:** Getting Started > Foundations (Everyone), https://agentfactory.panaversity.org/docs/foundations
**Platforms:** Instagram Reels + YouTube Shorts (ek hi vertical video dono jagah)
**Audience:** Pakistani beginners (students, freelancers, office workers, teachers, chhote business owners)
**Language:** Roman Urdu. Technical terms English mein hi rahenge (prompt, token, context window, Skill, Connector), kyunke audience yahi words tools mein dekhegi.
**Cadence:** 3 posts per week x 4 weeks = 12 posts

---

## Assumptions

User se sawal nahi poochay gaye, is liye yeh faislay khud liye hain:

1. **Posting days:** Monday, Wednesday, Friday. **Time:** raat 8:00 se 9:30 PKT. Is waqt log kaam aur classes se farigh hote hain. Do hafte baad analytics dekh kar time badal dein.
2. **Format:** 30 se 50 second ki vertical video, face-to-camera ya screen recording, aur on-screen Roman Urdu captions. Bohat se log bina awaaz ke dekhte hain, is liye captions zaroori hain.
3. **Ek post, ek idea.** Har post Foundations ke 10 crash courses mein se kisi ek ke ek concept par hai. Kisi bhi post mein poora course samjhane ki koshish nahi.
4. **CTA ka target:** pehle course page (free reading), phir Zia Tutor (kitab ka AI tutor), aur aakhri hafte mein PCAO-F certification. Beginners ko pehle hi din exam ki taraf nahi dhakelna.
5. **Link:** Instagram par "link in bio" (bio mein Foundations page ya Linktree). YouTube Shorts mein pinned comment aur description mein seedha course link.
6. **Facts:** har fact kitab ke System of Record se liya gaya hai (course ka slug har post mein diya hai). Koi bahar ka statistic nahi daala. Agar kitab update ho to record karne se pehle course page dobara check kar lein.
7. **Tools:** har demo free account par ho sakta hai (Claude ya ChatGPT). "Just Delegate It" course khud kehta hai ke browser mein free account kaafi hai.

## Week 1: AI hai kya? Darr khatam karo

### Post 1 (Mon): "Sawal mat poocho, kaam do"
- **Course:** Just Delegate It (`just-delegate-it-crash-course`)
- **Hook:** "Aap AI use nahi kar rahe. Aap sirf usse sawal pooch rahe ho, aur kaam abhi bhi aap ke paas hi hai."
- **Topic:** Sawal poochne aur kaam delegate karne mein farq. AI ko clicks aur steps mat batao, **outcome** batao. Ek line likho jo "Jab yeh kaam ho jayega, mere paas hoga..." se shuru ho. Phir do limits do, jaise "isi hafte shuru ho sake" aur "hafte mein 5 ghante se zyada nahi."
- **Demo idea:** Pehle step-by-step "recipe" wala prompt, phir ek-line outcome wala prompt. Dono ke results saath dikhao.
- **On-screen text:** "Steps nahi, OUTCOME do"
- **CTA:** "Comment mein apna ek kaam likho jo aap AI ko dena chahte ho. Hum agli video mein ek ka outcome-sentence bana denge. Poora course free hai, link bio mein."

### Post 2 (Wed): "AI librarian nahi, autocomplete hai"
- **Course:** What AI Actually Is (`what-ai-actually-is-crash-course`)
- **Hook:** "Aap samajhte ho AI kahin se facts dhoond kar laata hai? Bilkul ghalat. Aur zyada tar ghaltiyan isi soch se hoti hain."
- **Topic:** Language model agle text ka andaza lagata hai, ek token ke baad doosra. Yeh duniya ka sab se zyada parha likha autocomplete hai, encyclopedia nahi. "France ka capital" isliye sahi aata hai kyunke yeh baat lakhon dafa likhi gayi hai. Kisi nayi ya kam mashhoor cheez par yeh milti julti cheezein jor kar "sahi lagne wala" jawab bana deta hai.
- **On-screen text:** "Lookup nahi. PREDICTION."
- **CTA:** "Save kar lo, aur kisi aise dost ko bhejo jo AI ki har baat sach maan leta hai. Course: What AI Actually Is, link bio mein."

### Post 3 (Fri): "AI jhoot kyun bolta hai? Kyunke woh kharab nahi hai"
- **Course:** What AI Actually Is (`what-ai-actually-is-crash-course`)
- **Hook:** "AI ne aap ko confidently ghalat jawab diya? Woh glitch nahi tha. Machine bilkul waisa hi kaam kar rahi thi jaisi woh bani hai."
- **Topic:** Hallucination yani ek fluent, confident magar ghalat baat. Model ke andar koi alag hissa nahi jo check kare ke baat sach hai ya nahi. Uski training ek dafa hui aur phir freeze kar di gayi (knowledge cutoff), is liye usse aap ki private duniya ka pata nahi. Uska confident lehja ek andaaz hai, faisla nahi.
- **On-screen text:** "Confident lehja ≠ sach"
- **CTA:** "Comment mein batao: AI ne aap ko sab se funny ghalat jawab kya diya? Follow karo, Monday ko seekhenge ke AI se kaam kaise lete hain."

## Week 2: AI se baat karna seekho

### Post 4 (Mon): "Ek hi AI, do log, do alag results"
- **Course:** AI Fluency (`ai-fluency-crash-course`)
- **Hook:** "Do log, ek hi AI, ek hi subah. Ek ka kaam ready ho gaya, doosre ka kachre mein gaya. Farq tool mein nahi tha."
- **Topic:** AI access aur AI fluency do alag cheezein hain. **4Ds:** Delegation (faisla karo ke AI kya karega), Description (samjhao ke kya chahiye), Discernment (check karo jo mila), Diligence (zimmedari lo). Yeh framework Professor Rick Dakan aur Professor Joseph Feller ka hai, jo unhon ne Anthropic ke saath banaye courses mein diya.
- **On-screen text:** "Decide, Explain, Check, Own"
- **CTA:** "Is 4D checklist ka screenshot le lo aur agla kaam AI ko dene se pehle isse chala kar dekho. Poori checklist AI Fluency course mein hai, link bio mein."

### Post 5 (Wed): "AI aap ki tareef kyun karta rehta hai?"
- **Course:** AI Prompting in 2026 (`ai-prompting-2026`)
- **Hook:** "Apna business idea AI ko dikhaya aur usne kaha 'Zabardast!'? Woh sach nahi bol raha tha, woh aap ko khush kar raha tha."
- **Topic:** **Sycophancy** yani AI ki trained aadat ke woh wahi kahe jo aap sun-na chahte ho. Novice kehta hai "mera idea critique karo." Power user ek **rubric** deta hai: "Objectively analyze karo. Kya problem hal karne layak hai? Market hai? Competitive advantage hai?" Kitab ki misaal mein rubric ke saath AI ne ek idea ko 100 mein se 8 diye aur wajah bhi batayi.
- **Demo idea:** Kisi local idea ke saath dono prompt chala kar dikhao, jaise "ghar ka bana achaar online bechna."
- **On-screen text:** "Tareef nahi, RUBRIC maango"
- **CTA:** "Apna idea comment mein likho, hum usse rubric prompt ke saath test karenge. Pura prompting course, 13 concepts, link bio mein."

### Post 6 (Fri): "Google search ki tarah AI mat chalao"
- **Course:** AI Prompting in 2026 (`ai-prompting-2026`)
- **Hook:** "Zyada tar log AI ko Google ki tarah use karte hain: chhota sa sawal, jaldi se jawab, aage. Isi liye unhein 'slop' milta hai."
- **Topic:** **Slop** yani AI ka woh text jo upar se chikna magar andar se khali hota hai ("in today's fast-paced world..."). Isse bachne ka tareeqa: AI ko ek smart naye colleague ki tarah brief karo, files, context, constraints aur saaf ask ke saath. Ek line yaad rakho: **"Sahi context andar, ghalat context bahar."**
- **On-screen text:** "Right context IN, wrong context OUT"
- **CTA:** "Yeh line apne prompt notes mein save karo. Is hafte koi sawal ho to Zia Tutor se poochho, woh kitab ka AI tutor hai. Link bio mein."

## Week 3: AI se asal kaam karwao

### Post 7 (Mon): "Ek '#' aap ka prompt badal sakta hai"
- **Course:** Markdown In, HTML Out (`markdown-html-crash-course`)
- **Hook:** "Aap ka lamba paragraph wala prompt AI ke liye ulajhta hua dhaaga hai. Ek '#' aur ek '-' se yeh saaf ho jata hai."
- **Topic:** Jo cheez AI ko ghalat nahi karni, usse alag heading ya bullet do. Heading specific rakho: sirf "Budget" nahi, balke **"Budget: PKR 50,000 hard ceiling"** (yeh misaal kitab mein hai). Bullets (`-`) ka matlab ek set, numbers (`1.`) ka matlab tarteeb wale steps. Asool: **Markdown andar do, HTML wapas maango.** Faisla ek sawal se karo: isse aakhir mein kaun parhega?
- **On-screen text:** "Kaun parhega aakhir mein? Insaan = HTML, AI = Markdown"
- **CTA:** "Apna pichla prompt headings mein dobara likh kar try karo aur result comment mein batao. Course link bio mein."

### Post 8 (Wed): "Coding nahi aati? Koi masla nahi"
- **Course:** Code You Never Write (`code-you-never-write-crash-course`)
- **Hook:** "Ek bookkeeper ne kabhi code ki ek line nahi likhi. Phir bhi 1,400 rows mein 23 mismatches ek minute mein mil gaye."
- **Topic:** Masla saaf lafzon mein batao, AI khud code likhega, chalayega aur theek karega. Aap ko sirf check karna seekhna hai. Kab code chahiye: **Volume, Precision, Repetition, Files**, in mein se koi ek signal bhi ho. Warning wali misaal: ek school administrator ne fee spreadsheet AI ko di aur poocha kaun peeche hai. AI ne confidently 8 families batayin, asal mein 11 thi. Jis number par faisla hona hai, woh likha nahi, **calculate** hona chahiye.
- **Local angle:** teacher ke 200 students ke grades, dukaan ka monthly hisaab, freelancer ki invoices.
- **On-screen text:** "Number par faisla? Code chalwao."
- **CTA:** "Comment mein batao aap ka sab se boring Excel wala kaam kaunsa hai. Course 'Code You Never Write' free hai, link bio mein."

### Post 9 (Fri): "Har roz AI ko wohi baat samjhana band karo"
- **Course:** Skills & Connectors (`skills-connectors-crash-course`)
- **Hook:** "Aap AI ko vending machine ki tarah use kar rahe ho. Har roz wohi instructions dobara type, aur usse kuch yaad nahi rehta."
- **Topic:** Chat message AI ko *is dafa* batata hai. **Skill** usse sikhata hai ke kaam *har dafa* kaise karna hai, jaise recipe card. **Connector** AI ko aap ki asli apps tak "haath" deta hai: files, email, tracker. Kitchen wali misaal: Connectors kitchen hain, Skills recipe cards. Taste check karna phir bhi aap ki zimmedari hai.
- **On-screen text:** "Skill = recipe card. Connector = kitchen."
- **CTA:** "Follow karo. Agle hafte seekhenge ke AI par andha bharosa kyun nahi karna. Skills & Connectors course mein koding zero hai, link bio mein."

## Week 4: Samajhdar aur zimmedar user bano

### Post 10 (Mon): "Saaf likha hua ≠ sahi likha hua"
- **Course:** How to Think in the AI Era (`how-to-think-ai-era`)
- **Hook:** "AI ka jawab itna professional lagta hai ke aap bina parhe forward kar dete ho. Yahi sab se khatarnak lamha hai."
- **Topic:** AI ghalat hote hue bhi confident aur professional lagta hai. Aadat: AI ke har claim par likho "agree, disagree, ya kuch miss hua?" aur saath mein ek line ki wajah. Agar aap har baat se agree ho, to aap ne kaafi socha nahi. Best result tab aata hai jab aap sochte aur faisla karte ho, aur AI research aur drafting karta hai. Ulta karoge to aap ki zaroorat hi khatam ho jayegi.
- **On-screen text:** "Pehle khud socho. Phir AI se compare karo."
- **CTA:** "Agla AI jawab bhejne se pehle 'agree / disagree / miss' wala test karo. Save kar lo taake yaad rahe. Course link bio mein."

### Post 11 (Wed): "Achha output ka matlab yeh nahi ke step safe hai"
- **Course:** Workflow Design & Diagnosis (`workflow-design-diagnosis-crash-course`)
- **Hook:** "Do teams, ek hi AI, ek hi kaam. Ek ka review time aadha ho gaya. Doosri ne ek mahine mein tool hi band kar diya."
- **Topic:** Contract review wali kahani. Team 1 ne kaam step-by-step map kiya aur har change par lawyer ki approval rakhi. Team 2 ne poora process AI ko de diya aur low-risk clauses bina nigrani ke approve hone diye, phir ek clause ne aisi obligation bana di jo kisi ne nahi pakdi. Sabaq: **"Output verdict nahi hota."** Har step ko is se parkho ke ghalti ki keemat kya hai aur jawab kaun dega. Aur jab kaam bigre to dekho kab se bigra.
- **Local angle:** office, agency ya freelancing team jahan AI client ko jaane wala kaam bana raha hai.
- **On-screen text:** "Output is NOT the verdict"
- **CTA:** "Apni team ke saath share karo jo AI ko 'sab kuch' dene wali hai. Is hafte ka koi sawal Zia Tutor se poochho, link bio mein."

### Post 12 (Fri): "AI mein kya paste NAHI karna" + series wrap
- **Course:** Governance, Risk & Responsible Use (`governance-risk-responsible-use-crash-course`)
- **Hook:** "Customer list, passwords, patient records... aap ne inmein se kuch AI mein paste kiya hai? Ruk jao."
- **Topic:** Tarteeb: **data ki category tay karo, phir dekho ke task ko pehchaan wali details chahiye ya nahi, phir route confirm karo, phir control chuno.** Credentials aur API secrets: red. Patient, financial ya privileged records: red, jab tak organization ne woh route approve na kiya ho. Spending trends ke liye pattern chahiye, log nahi, is liye identifiers hata do. AI ko "yeh data yaad mat rakhna" kehna sirf ek khwahish hai, control nahi. Aakhri 8 se 10 second: 4 hafte ka recap (Delegate, Samjho, Prompt, Kaam, Zimmedari).
- **On-screen text:** "Khwahish ≠ control"
- **CTA (do hisson mein):**
  1. "Poora Foundations track free hai. Link bio mein, aaj se shuru karo."
  2. "Agar aap AI se roz kaam lete ho aur proof chahiye ke aap achha aur sirf achha-lagne wala output alag kar sakte ho, to Panaversity ka **PCAO-F** exam dekho. Yeh FDE path ka pehla exam hai, aur Panaversity students ko do attempts free milte hain."

## Production aur posting notes

- **Captions/description template:**
  `[Hook ki line] | [2-3 line summary Roman Urdu mein] | Course: [naam] (free) | Link: bio / pinned comment | #AISeekho #PakistanAI #UrduAI #PanaversityAgentFactory #AIForBeginners`
- **Series branding:** har video ke kone mein "Foundations 1/12, 2/12..." ka label, taake log poori series dhoondein. Instagram par 4 hafton ka ek Highlight ya Guide, aur YouTube par "AI Seekho" naam ki Shorts playlist.
- **Comment-driven follow-ups:** Posts 1, 5 aur 8 ke CTA comments maangte hain. Sab se achhe comment ka jawab video-reply (Reels) ya Short-reply ke taur par do. Yeh in 12 posts ke ilawa bonus content hai.
- **Ek sentence ek line:** captions mein lambi English terms ke saath ek baar Roman Urdu wazahat do, jaise "hallucination, yani confident jhoot."
- **Accuracy check:** Posts 5 aur 8 ke numbers (8/100 aur 8 vs 11) aur Post 12 mein PCAO-F ke "do free attempts" record karne se pehle course aur certification page par dobara check karo. Kitab update hoti rehti hai.

## Kya measure karna hai

- **Week 1 se 2:** saves aur shares, kyunke yeh dikhata hai ke beginners ke liye content kaam ka hai.
- **Week 3:** comments, yani log apne kaam share kar rahe hain ya nahi.
- **Week 4:** link clicks (bio aur pinned comment), aur PCAO-F wale page par traffic.
- 2 hafte baad: jis course wali video ki retention sab se zyada ho, us course ki agli series mein 2 se 3 aur posts banao.
